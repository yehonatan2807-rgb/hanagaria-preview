A family workshop, from the first meeting to the last screw

HaNagaria in Kfar Adumim is run by Shmulik Tawil. Custom furniture for homes and businesses: kitchens, wardrobes, bookcases and complete projects.

Shelly Ben David, the workshop's interior designer and Shmulik's daughter, accompanies every project from the first meeting to installation.

Production, hardware and painting are done in-house with modern equipment. Installation is done by a permanent team with a dedicated lead.
